CYBERSECURITY ESSENTIALS

What are Phishing Emails?

Phishing is a social engineering attack where malicious actors impersonate reputable institutions—such as banks, universities, tech corporations, or executive colleagues—to deceive recipients into disclosing confidential information like passwords, credit card numbers, or two-factor authentication tokens.

Psychological Urgency & Fear

Attackers manufacture artificial deadlines ("Account suspended in 24 hours") to bypass rational critical thinking.

Deceptive Domain Spoofing

Attackers substitute subtle homoglyphs (e.g. uppercase 'I' for lowercase 'l' in paypaI) or use shady TLDs.

Harvesting Exploitations

Victims are funneled to cloned portals designed to siphon authentication credentials directly into attacker servers.

Machine Learning vs. Static Keyword Filters

Traditional email filters rely heavily on static blacklists and rigid keyword rules which attackers easily circumvent by inserting typos, emojis, or zero-width spaces. PhishGuard AI employs probabilistic machine learning:

  • TF-IDF Vectorization: Computes the statistical rarity and significance of terms across legitimate and malicious corpuses.
  • Multinomial Naive Bayes: Calculates conditionally independent joint probability distributions across the entire email document.
  • Heuristic Triangulation: Supplements statistical inference with targeted checks for domain typosquatting and unencrypted endpoints.
