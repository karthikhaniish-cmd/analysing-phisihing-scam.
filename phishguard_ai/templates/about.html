About PhishGuard AI

An engineering and data science research project exploring machine learning for automated email threat classification.

Project Abstract

Phishing attacks continue to represent over 80% of reported enterprise security compromises. Malicious actors exploit human vulnerability through coercive psychological tactics, spoofed administrative headers, and weaponized hyperlinks.

PhishGuard AI demonstrates an end-to-end detection pipeline combining Natural Language Processing (NLP) feature extraction with supervised probabilistic modeling. By analyzing statistical distributions of unigrams, bigrams, and syntactic heuristics, the system calculates precise threat likelihoods without executing suspicious attachments or visiting adversarial endpoints.

1. TF-IDF Feature Extraction

Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) converts unstructured email text into a numerical vector space. It balances local frequency with global corpus rarity:

$$ \text{TF-IDF}(t, d, D) = \text{TF}(t, d) \times \log\left(\frac{1 + |D|}{1 + \text{DF}(t, D)}\right) + 1 $$
  • TF (Term Frequency): Frequency of token $t$ inside document $d$ (using sublinear logarithmic scaling).
  • IDF (Inverse Document Frequency): Penalizes ubiquitous terms like "the" or "and" while elevating discriminative tokens like "ssn", "verify", or "suspended".

2. Multinomial Naive Bayes

Multinomial Naive Bayes computes the maximum a posteriori (MAP) class assignment given the bag-of-words representation under the conditional independence assumption:

$$ P(c | d) \propto P(c) \prod_{i=1}^{n} P(w_i | c)^{\text{TF-IDF}(w_i, d)} $$
  • Prior Probability $P(c)$: The base distribution of phishing vs legitimate emails in the corpus.
  • Laplace Smoothing ($\alpha = 0.1$): Mitigates zero-frequency pitfalls for novel words encountered at inference time.

Heuristic Warning Signs & Threat Taxonomy

In addition to statistical learning, PhishGuard AI evaluates transparent, explainable heuristic patterns:

Threat Vector Observed Pattern Typical Attacker Goal Severity
Psychological Coercion "Urgent action required within 24 hours", "Account deletion" Force impulsive compliance before security verification High
Credential Harvesting Explicit requests for PIN, password, SSN, seed phrase Direct account takeover and identity theft High
Typosquatting & Homoglyphs paypaI.com (capital i), micro-soft.xyz, bofa-cardprotect.cc Deceive users into trusting spoofed login destinations High
Insecure Protocol Unencrypted HTTP links instead of valid HTTPS Man-in-the-middle credential interception Medium
Domain Authority Mismatch Bank or payroll claims sent from free @gmail or @yahoo mailbox Bypassing domain registration or spoofing filters High
Ethical Considerations & Safe Analysis Guarantee

PhishGuard AI adheres to strict safe-handling practices: submitted email contents are analyzed strictly in memory, URLs are parsed using regular expression tokenizers without performing HTTP network requests or DNS resolutions, and no attachments or executable macros are ever evaluated.
